Features

Two campaigns

Play the Resistance (Liberation of Peru) and the Global Confederation, or set up a skirmish against computer opponents of several difficulty levels.

Multiplayer for up to 8

PC, Android and iOS in one match: LAN discovery, direct connection or rooms on the public server joined by a short code. Custom maps transfer automatically.

Classic RTS gameplay

Base building, power and economy, research, fog of war, control groups, attack-move and siege mode for snipers and artillery.

Mouse, keyboard and touch

Familiar desktop controls on PC; a landscape touch interface with gestures and contextual orders on phones and tablets.

Graphics settings

High resolutions and fullscreen, zoom, day, dusk and night lighting, weapon and explosion glow, crisp pixel art.

Editors, mods, replays

Map and unit editors, mod support, and every network match saved as a replay you can watch later.

Controls

Mouse and keyboard, PC version.

LMBDrag

Select units

Click or drag a box. Double-click selects all units of that type.

RMB

Move and attack

Move units, attack a target or set a factory rally point.

Ctrl1…0

Control groups

Ctrl + digit assigns a group, the digit selects it, pressing it twice centres the camera.

QLMB

Attack-move

Units march to the point and engage every enemy on the way.

G

Siege mode

Long-range stance for snipers and Rhino heavy artillery.

WASDWheel

Camera and zoom

Pan with WASD, arrow keys or the held middle button; the wheel zooms.

Radar+ / −

Minimap

Click the radar to jump there; scroll over it to zoom.

F11PEsc

Screen and pause

F11 fullscreen, P pause in a network match, Esc settings menu.

On Android and iOS: tap to select, tap the ground to move, tap again to attack-move, drag with one finger to pan.

Server and rooms

About the project

I have long been a fan of classic strategy games like Red Alert 1 and 2. Back in the days of push-button Nokia phones, my friends and I spent hours playing Art of War 2: Liberation of Peru, discussing tactics and planning tank assaults.

For years I hoped to see this series on PC. There is a third installment, but the second one is where I still feel the real classic RTS: careful balance, a fair economy and the thrill of every battle.

Inspired by Warzone 2100 and OpenRA, I started this project to give the game a second life on big screens with proper controls and multiplayer. Sincere thanks to Gear Games and everyone who made the game we grew up with.
